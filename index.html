01 / OUR MISSION

A new language
for cellular
communication.

Designing proteins to understand, control, and reprogram cellular signaling.

At the Wang Lab, we combine artificial intelligence, computational protein design, and experimental biology to develop synthetic proteins that precisely control cellular signaling. Our goal is to uncover fundamental principles of receptor activation and translate these insights into new molecular tools and therapeutics.

DESIGN→UNDERSTAND→TRANSLATE

02 / RESEARCH AREAS

Built from possibility.
Driven by biology.

Four connected directions.
One shared vision for protein design.

01DESIGN

De Novo
Protein Design

We combine artificial intelligence, computational modeling, and protein engineering to design novel proteins with tailored structures and functions, expanding the possibilities of what proteins can do.

02PROGRAM

Programming
Cell Signaling

We develop synthetic proteins to precisely control receptor interactions, assembly, and activation, enabling programmable regulation of cellular signaling pathways.

03DISCOVER

Molecular Toolbox
Development

We create versatile molecular tools to probe protein interactions, manipulate signaling pathways, and uncover fundamental principles of cellular communication.

04TRANSLATE

Therapeutics
Development

We translate advances in protein design into next-generation therapeutics, engineering proteins that selectively modulate disease-associated signaling pathways.
